# Clause 5.3 — PWHT

Section 5 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 12

**Appendix F approval class:** A (approval_required = true)

## Atomic rules

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.3:R01

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** AS 4458
- **cross_references:** Appendix F

> PWHT is required per AS 4458, the line spec or isometrics; stress-reduction PWHT requires Jemena approval.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.3:R02

- **modality:** shall
- **cross_references:** Appendix F

> A written PWHT procedure and weld procedure are to be submitted for approval.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.3:R03

- **modality:** shall

> Sufficient thermocouples are to be used; machined surfaces are to be protected.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.3:R04

- **modality:** not_permitted

> No manual gas torches, gas rings or exothermic kits are permitted; rigid support and venting are required; PTFE pads are to be protected.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.3:R05

- **modality:** not_permitted

> PWHT Reports contents are specified; no welding or heating is permitted after final PWHT; NDE is to be performed after final PWHT.
