# Clause 5.2.2 — Weld Spacing and Location

Section 5 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 10

## Atomic rules (verbatim)

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.2.2:R01

- **modality:** shall

> Longitudinal seams are to be offset a minimum of 90° or 250 mm whichever is smaller.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.2.2:R02

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** AS 2885.2 Clause 5.13 - Distance between welds

> The minimum girth-weld distance is 1.5 x pipe diameter; approval is required if this is not met.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.2.2:R03

- **modality:** informational

> The minimum branch-weld distance is 6 x wall thickness; closer if PWHT.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.2.2:R04

- **modality:** not_permitted

> Branch and non-pressure welds are not to cross seams or butt welds, with toe-to-toe separation; avoid more than 2 weld intersections.
