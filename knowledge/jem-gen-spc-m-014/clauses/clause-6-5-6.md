# Clause 6.5.6 — Weld Interruption

Section 6 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 27

## Atomic rules

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:6.5.6:R01

- **modality:** shall

> Welding is to be continuous except for de-slag/electrode change; indefinite interruption is permitted after root + first fill for DN150 and 15 mm wall and smaller, or where throat ≥15 mm with no preheat; re-inspect visual + MPI and re-establish preheat before resuming.
