# Clause 5.4.7 — Instrument Tubing/Fittings

Section 5 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 17

**Appendix F approval class:** C (approval_required = true)

## Atomic rules

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.4.7:R01

- **modality:** shall

> Instrument tubing/fittings are to be per detail drawings.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.4.7:R02

- **modality:** shall

> An approved cutter is to be used and the tube reamed; no hacksaw; approved bending machines; true vertical/horizontal; close ends; cable-tray routing with staggered joints.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.4.7:R03

- **modality:** shall
- **cross_references:** Appendix A; Appendix F

> Swagelok compression fittings are to be used with a gap gauge; AGA-approved thread sealant; discrepancies via TQ form; clean pneumatic tubing.
