# Clause 7.3.2 — Radiography

Section 7 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 32

**Appendix F approval class:** B (approval_required = true)

## Atomic rules

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:7.3.2:R01

- **modality:** may
- **cross_references:** Appendix F

> 100% of the nominated weld length is radiographed; UT may be used instead if configuration precludes radiography and Jemena accepts.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:7.3.2:R02

- **modality:** shall

> X-ray fine-grain film is to be used; x-ray is preferred for ≤25 mm wall, gamma case-by-case; radiographic procedures are to be developed.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:7.3.2:R03

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** AS 2177

> A source-side wire IQI per AS2177 is used; single wall single image; lead screens and fine-grain high-contrast film.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:7.3.2:R04

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** AS 4037

> Minimum film density is 1.7 through the weld and parent 2.5–4.0 for x-ray; no fluorescent screens; sensitivity/unsharpness per AS4037.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:7.3.2:R05

- **modality:** shall

> Qualification radiographs are retained free of masking defects; lead-character ID content a–f; other NDE for clarification; a radiation safety officer, regulations and signage/barriers are required.
