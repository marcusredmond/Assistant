# Clause 5.2.3 — Branch Connections

Section 5 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 11

## Atomic rules

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.2.3:R01

- **modality:** shall

> Branch connection type/size is to be per the branch chart; bore edges are to be radiused.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.2.3:R02

- **modality:** shall

> DN 25 and below are to be drilled and de-burred.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.2.3:R03

- **modality:** shall

> Reinforcement pads are to have a ¼" NPT plugged hole.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.2.3:R04

- **modality:** shall

> Structural/trunnion pads are to have one 6mm vent hole sealed with mastic.
