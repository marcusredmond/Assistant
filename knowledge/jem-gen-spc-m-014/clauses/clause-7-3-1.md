# Clause 7.3.1 — Non Destructive Examination - General

Section 7 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 31

**Appendix F approval class:** A (approval_required = true)

## Atomic rules

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:7.3.1:R01

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** AS 4037
- **cross_references:** Appendix F

> The Contractor provides equipment/services; all pressure welds are NDE per the project spec; acceptance is per AS4037 Very Harmful Gas fluid service; NDE is 100% unless specified otherwise, per AS4037.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:7.3.1:R02

- **modality:** shall
- **cross_references:** Appendix F

> Records are provided to Jemena; the NDE written practice is endorsed; licensed personnel acceptable to Jemena, a NATA-accredited company and NATA signatory are required.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:7.3.1:R03

- **modality:** shall

> Welds are sentenced C/DNC with a report; % welding and repair rate are tracked; for <100% NDE, equal coverage per welder/operator/procedure is required; X-Ray is required, Gamma case-by-case.
