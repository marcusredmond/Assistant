# Clause 4.2 — Welding Consumables

Section 4 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 8

**Appendix F approval class:** B (approval_required = true)

## Atomic rules

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:4.2:R01

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** AWS A5.9; AWS A5.18

> GMAW consumables are to comply with AWS A5.9 or A5.18.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:4.2:R02

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** AWS A5.20

> GSFCAW consumables are to comply with AWS A5.20.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:4.2:R03

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** AWS A5.18

> GTAW consumables are to comply with AWS A5.18.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:4.2:R04

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** AWS A5.17; AWS A5.23

> SAW Wire & Flux are to comply with AWS A5.17 or A5.23.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:4.2:R05

- **modality:** shall
- **cross_references:** Appendix F

> Shielding Gases are to be Jemena-approved.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:4.2:R06

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** AWS A5.1; AWS A5.5

> MMAW consumables are to comply with AWS A5.1 or A5.5.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:4.2:R07

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** AWS A5.20; AWS A5.29

> FCAW consumables are to comply with AWS A5.20 or A5.29.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:4.2:R08

- **modality:** shall

> Consumables are to be batch tested and supplied with certificates, in sealed factory packaging.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:4.2:R09

- **modality:** shall

> Weld-metal properties are to be similar to the base; the higher grade governs transition welds; impact test properties are to match.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:4.2:R10

- **modality:** shall
- **cross_references:** 11

> Storage and handling procedures are to be in accordance with Section 11, with moisture/contamination control, segregated storage and the manufacturer's pre-dry instructions.
