# Clause 5.4.1 — Shop Installation - General

Section 5 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 13

**Appendix F approval class:** A (approval_required = true)

## Atomic rules

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.4.1:R01

- **modality:** shall
- **cross_references:** Appendix F

> Internal inspection and cleaning are required; installation is to be per drawings with no changes without Jemena written permission.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.4.1:R02

- **modality:** shall

> Work is to be neat and to good practice, true vertical/horizontal, avoiding residual stress with no springing, forcing, misalignment or cold pull.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.4.1:R03

- **modality:** shall

> Permanent supports are to be fitted where practicable; swab clean before tie-in; protect inline fittings and cover open ends.
