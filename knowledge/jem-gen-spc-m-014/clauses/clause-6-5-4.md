# Clause 6.5.4 — Alignment

Section 6 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 26

## Atomic rules

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:6.5.4:R01

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** AS 4458

> Line-up clamps/bridging are to be used with tacks in the bevel; no strong-backs; misalignment per AS4458; no temporary attachments.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:6.5.4:R02

- **modality:** shall

> Stainless tack bridging is to use the root-pass process/consumables.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:6.5.4:R03

- **modality:** shall

> DN 80+ require a minimum of four 30 mm tacks equidistant; remove all tacks; bridge tacks; grind and NDE cracked tacks.
