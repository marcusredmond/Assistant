# Clause 5.4.2 — Flanged Connections

Section 5 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 14

**Appendix F approval class:** A (approval_required = true)

## Atomic rules

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.4.2:R01

- **modality:** shall

> Flanged connections are to be per the class datasheet with new gaskets/bolting and washers.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.4.2:R02

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** GAS-980-PR-ME-003 - Bolt Torque & Tensioning Procedure
- **cross_references:** Appendix F

> A Bolt Torque & Tensioning Procedure per GAS-980-PR-ME-003 is to be submitted for approval.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.4.2:R03

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** GAS-980-PR-ME-003

> Thread lubricant is to be used with uniform diametric tightening, flanges parallel, and ≥3 free threads per GAS-980-PR-ME-003.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.4.2:R04

- **modality:** shall

> Damaged gaskets are to be replaced at no cost; faces are to be clean/undamaged, parallel/centred, with no excessive force.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.4.2:R05

- **modality:** shall

> Spiral wound gaskets are to be to guide-ring thickness; no gasket re-use and no sealing compound.
