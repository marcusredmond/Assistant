# Clause 6.3.1 — WPQT

Section 6 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 21

## Atomic rules

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:6.3.1:R01

- **modality:** not_permitted
- **cross_references:** 6.3.2; Appendix F

> No repair of qualification welds is permitted; calibrated equipment with certificates reviewed; a qualified monitor; the Contractor is responsible for NDE/destructive testing per 6.3.2.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:6.3.1:R02

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** AS/NZS 3992; AS 4041
- **cross_references:** Appendix F

> Test welds are to be cleared by NDE before mechanical testing unless Jemena approves otherwise; mechanical testing is per AS3992/AS4041.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:6.3.1:R03

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** ASTM A106 Gr. B; AS 4041

> Charpy of ASTM A106 Gr. B is tested at minimum design temperature, evaluated per AS4041; mechanical testing is by an independent NATA lab.
