# Clause 5.2.5 — Dimension and Tolerance

Section 5 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 11

## Atomic rules (verbatim)

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.2.5:R01

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** AS 4041; AS 2885

> Dimensions and tolerances are to be in accordance with AS4041 or AS 2885.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.2.5:R02

- **modality:** may

> Linear tolerance is ±1.5 mm for spools ≤1.5 m and ±3.0 mm for spools >1.5 m; closer tolerances may be specified; tolerances are not cumulative.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.2.5:R03

- **modality:** informational

> Angular tolerance is ±0.50°.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.2.5:R04

- **modality:** shall

> Flange bolt-hole misalignment is to be ≤1.5 mm and to straddle centre lines ±0.50°.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.2.5:R05

- **modality:** shall

> Flange face offset from square is to be ≤¼° or 1 mm whichever is smaller; mating flanges are to be parallel.
