# Clause 1.2 — Scope

Section 1 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 1

## Atomic rules

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:1.2:R01

- **modality:** informational

> This Specification sets out the minimum technical and material requirements for the shop fabrication, welding, heat treatment, coating, examination, testing and cleaning of piping and mechanical equipment.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:1.2:R02

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** AS 4041

> This Specification is to be used in conjunction with AS 4041 where specified.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:1.2:R03

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** AS 4041; AS 2885

> Materials are to be in accordance with AS 4041 or AS 2885 as specified.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:1.2:R04

- **modality:** shall
- **cross_references:** Appendix A

> Exceptions to this Specification are to be requested via the Technical Query form in Appendix A.
