# Clause 5.4.5 — Supports

Section 5 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 16

## Atomic rules

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.4.5:R01

- **modality:** shall

> Supports are to be per project drawings with no welding except where shown.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.4.5:R02

- **modality:** shall

> Attachments are to be of parent material with non-penetrating welds by qualified welders; reinforcement pads are from header pipe; no load gaps.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:5.4.5:R03

- **modality:** shall

> Vibration supports are per detail; anti-friction pads are to be used.
