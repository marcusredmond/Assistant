# Clause 6.5.1 — Production Welding - General

Section 6 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 24

## Atomic rules

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:6.5.1:R01

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** AS 4458

> Production welding is to be per AS4458, approved procedures and approved welders.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:6.5.1:R02

- **modality:** shall

> Welds are to be protected from wind/rain; no backing rings; no lift until 50% weld depth; re-PWHT if welding after PWHT.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:6.5.1:R03

- **modality:** shall
- **cross_references:** Appendix B; 5.2

> O-let welds are to be a concave fillet with the toe ground smooth (see Appendix B); each pass is to be cleaned; no quenching; clean surfaces; FFW per 5.2; refrain in bad weather; no arc burns.
