# Clause 11.1 — Storage Handling and Protection

Section 11 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 48

**Appendix F approval class:** A (approval_required = true)

## Atomic rules (verbatim)

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:11.1:R01

- **modality:** informational
- **cross_references:** Appendix F

> A storage/handling procedure is submitted for approval to prevent mechanical/coating damage, weathering and theft.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:11.1:R02

- **modality:** informational

> Minimise pipe flex; no dropping/dragging/skidding; no metal bolsters/chain wrenches.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:11.1:R03

- **modality:** informational

> Valves are stored until spools are ready, preventing moisture ingress; globe/gate stored closed, ball/plug fully open; seal open ends.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:11.1:R04

- **modality:** informational

> Bolting is stored under cover with lubricant; mask stainless/nameplates/threads/machined surfaces, oil non-stainless; segregate grades.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:11.1:R05

- **modality:** informational
- **cross_references:** Appendix F

> Protect flanges/weld preps/threads; store uncoated flanges/gaskets/bolts indoors dry; use non-metallic or coated slings; damaged materials are replaced/repaired at Contractor cost.
