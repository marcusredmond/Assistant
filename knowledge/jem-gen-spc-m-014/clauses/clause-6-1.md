# Clause 6.1 — General

Section 6 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 19

**Appendix F approval class:** A (approval_required = true)

## Atomic rules (verbatim)

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:6.1:R01

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** AS 4041; AS/NZS 3992; AS/NZS 1554

> Welding is to be in accordance with AS4041, AS3992 and this specification; structural welding per AS1554.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:6.1:R02

- **modality:** may
- **cross_references:** Appendix F

> Previously qualified procedures may be accepted; WPS/WPQR are to be submitted for approval; a repair-welding procedure is required.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:6.1:R03

- **modality:** may

> Products are to be within manufacturer limits; Jemena may revoke.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:6.1:R04

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** AS/NZS 3992

> Welds commenced the same day are to be completed the same day; WPS essential variables are per AS3992.
