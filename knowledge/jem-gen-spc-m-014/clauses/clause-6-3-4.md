# Clause 6.3.4 — Repair Procedures

Section 6 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 23

## Atomic rules (verbatim)

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:6.3.4:R01

- **modality:** shall

> Detailed WPSs for repair are required per material/thickness, with a defect removal method statement.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:6.3.4:R02

- **modality:** informational

> Repair WPS qualification is not required for carbon steel if the same WPS is used.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:6.3.4:R03

- **modality:** shall

> Only one repair per weld joint is permitted, else a new joint is required.
