# Clause 3.2 — Material Control

Section 3 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 6

**Appendix F approval class:** A (approval_required = true)

## Atomic rules (verbatim)

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:3.2:R01

- **modality:** shall

> Material control procedures are to be in place before work commences; traceable certification is to be retained in the MDR.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:3.2:R02

- **modality:** informational

> Material control covers receipt/verification; sub-contractor traceability; consumable traceability; handling; storage; marking/identification; quarantine; and a materials register subject to audit.
