# Clause 3.3.1 — Marking, Identification and Traceability - General Requirements

Section 3 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 6

## Atomic rules

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:3.3.1:R01

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** AS 4458

> Marking and identification are to be in accordance with AS4458.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:3.3.1:R02

- **modality:** shall

> Unique spool numbers are to be assigned and recorded on the isometric.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:3.3.1:R03

- **modality:** shall

> Paint markers are to be free of sulphur, chlorides and halogens.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:3.3.1:R04

- **modality:** shall
- **cross_references:** 5.3

> For PWHT, paint is to be less than 250ppm lead, zinc, tin, copper and titanium-oxide.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:3.3.1:R05

- **modality:** shall

> Offcut identification is to be transferred by metal disc stamping.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:3.3.1:R06

- **modality:** shall

> Unidentifiable material is to be rejected and markings maintained.
