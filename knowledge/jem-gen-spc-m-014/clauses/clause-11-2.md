# Clause 11.2 — Packing and Transport

Section 11 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 50

**Appendix F approval class:** A (approval_required = true)

## Atomic rules

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:11.2:R01

- **modality:** shall
- **cross_references:** Appendix F

> Packing is for outdoor unprotected storage; a preservation/protection procedure is submitted for approval.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:11.2:R02

- **modality:** shall

> Transport the full skid with supports/base frame where possible, bolted down, with loose items secured; preserve during storage/fabrication/load-out; seal all openings; apply rust preventative on exposed machined surfaces; hard-stamp-tag loose flanges.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:11.2:R03

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** API 6D Section 8

> Pack loose spools/valves/bolts/gaskets in boxes with no mixed metals per crate; loose valves per API 6D Section 8 additions: plug apertures/vents; no tack-welding protectors; moisture-resistant rigid crates with waterproof plastic envelope, marked per PO; hard-copy certification shipped with the equipment.
