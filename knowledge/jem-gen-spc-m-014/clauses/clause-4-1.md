# Clause 4.1 — General

Section 4 · JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014 v4 · source page 8

**Appendix F approval class:** C (approval_required = true)

## Atomic rules

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:4.1:R01

- **modality:** shall
- **references:** AS 4041; AS 2885

> Materials are to be in accordance with AS4041 or AS 2885.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:4.1:R02

- **modality:** shall

> Materials are to be new, unused and defect-free.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:4.1:R03

- **modality:** shall
- **cross_references:** Appendix F

> Attachments are to be of the same material as the parent pipe unless Jemena approves otherwise.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:4.1:R04

- **modality:** shall

> Material is to be quarantined until identified; Mill Certificates are required.

### JEM-GEN-SPC-M-014:4.1:R05

- **modality:** informational

> Temporary materials, including hydrotest gaskets, are addressed.
